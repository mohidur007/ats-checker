    What this checker looks at

    Keyword matchSkills and terms from the job description that appear in your resume.
    Standard sectionsExperience, Education, Skills and Summary under familiar headings.
    Contact infoName, email and phone that a parser can find.
    ParsabilityReadable text, no tables or odd symbols, single column.
    Content qualityAction verbs, measurable results and bullet points.
    Length and datesSensible length and one consistent date format.
    Is this a real ATS score?

    No. Real systems do not publish a score. This is an estimate from readability and keyword rules, so use it as a guide.

    What is a good score here?

    75 and above means the resume is readable and well matched. Below 55 means there are clear fixes to make.

    Does it work offline?
